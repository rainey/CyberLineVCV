# Heart — user manual

`cyberline-heart` models a mammalian heart as a physical system: an
**atrium** that collects venous return, a **ventricle** whose muscular
wall contracts around whatever it was filled with, and an elastic
**artery** (the aorta) that smooths each pulse into continuous pressure.
Everything the module outputs — envelopes, clocks, ECG, heartbeat sounds,
murmurs — is generated by the same four physics rules. Nothing is a
"curve menu" wearing a biology costume.

```
venous return ──▶ [atrium] ──valve──▶ [ventricle] ──valve──▶ [artery] ──▶ body
                     ▲                    ▲                   │
                  SA node ──AV delay──► contraction          Windkessel
                  (the clock)            twitch              runoff
```

## Quick start: the heartbeat

1. Patch **SOUND** into your output chain (it's an audio signal — normal
   level, apply your own gain).
2. That's it. The SA node free-runs at 72 bpm; you hear the "lub-dub":
   **S1** is the mitral valve slamming shut at the start of systole (low
   thump), **S2** is the aortic valve closing at the end (higher, shorter).

## The outputs, as instruments

| Output | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **VOLUME** | Ventricular volume. Slow fill, a held breath (isovolumetric contraction — volume frozen while pressure climbs), a fast decay, another freeze. | The signature asymmetric envelope: pad swells, plucked decays. Patch to a VCA or filter CV. |
| **PRESSURE** | Ventricular pressure — the same beat seen from the force side: rises while VOLUME is frozen, falls during relaxation. | Inverted/complementary envelopes; duo with VOLUME for "two faces of one event" morphing. |
| **FLOW** | Signed CV: **+ while filling, − while ejecting** (the sign carries the physical direction — same convention as Breath's FLOW). | Bipolar modulation, sign-as-gate patching (rectify → fill/eject gate). |
| **OUTFLOW** | Unipolar pulse per beat, tracking instantaneous aortic flow (its integral over the beat = stroke volume). | Sidechain-style pumps; **the series-link jack** (below). |
| **ARTERIAL** | Pressure in the aorta: steps up during ejection, decays exponentially between beats. | A natural "sustain-then-decay" envelope; the pulse-pressure shape (rise width vs decay) is the afterload character. |
| **ECG** | The electrical waveform: P (atrial), QRS (ventricular spike), T (repolarization bump). R ≈ 3× the T wave, T ≈ 1.8× the P wave, widths scale with the beat. | Spiky slow LFO; sync source; a "medical monitor" visual when scoped. |
| **BEAT** | 5 V trigger at ventricular activation (the R spike). | Master clock: everything downstream lands on the "lub." |
| **ATRIA** | 5 V trigger at atrial activation — earlier than BEAT by the AV delay. | The swing/offbeat partner: sequence hats or accents on ATRIA against BEAT. |
| **SOUND** | Phonocardiogram (audio): S1/S2 thumps, S3/S4 when the physics allows them, murmur noise when valves are diseased. | Instant heartbeat; percussion voice with mass-scaled pitch. |

**The heart is a clock.** BEAT/ATRIA are the clean triggers, but every
other output is synchronized by construction — one beat, five synchronous
views. No clock/scaling gymnastics needed.

## The knobs, as anatomy

Every knob moves anatomy or physiology, never a resonance or a curve:

- **HEART MASS** — the allometric master knob (log scale, 5 g → 2000 kg,
  human = 70 kg = 12 o'clock-ish default 0.74). One knob gives you the
  whole mammal family: **rate ∝ M^(−1/4)** (shrew/mouse ~600 bpm, human 72,
  elephant ~30), **stroke volume ∝ M**, **AV delay ∝ M^(1/4)**, and the
  **S1/S2 thump pitch ∝ M^(−1/3)** — small hearts tick fast and high, big
  hearts boom slow and deep, like drum sizes. Blood *pressure* is
  size-invariant in nature, so the pressure outputs do not rescale —
  only time and volume do. (Mass display in kg.)
- **RATE** — the intrinsic SA-node rate at human anatomy (30–220 bpm);
  mass, autonomic drive and HRV multiply it. This is a *base rate*, not a
  forced tempo: DRIVE can push the effective rate far past the knob.
- **AV DELAY** — the PR interval (0.06–0.25 s): the time the atrial
  contraction leads the ventricular one. The built-in swing between the
  ATRIA and BEAT clocks. Auto-clamped to 30% of the cycle at high rates
  (sympathetic conduction speeds up — the atrial kick survives tachycardia).
- **FILL** — venous return (preload). By Frank–Starling's law the
  ventricle contracts harder when stretched fuller: stroke volume, S1
  loudness and VOLUME depth all rise together. Emergent, not scripted.
- **DRIVE** — autonomic tone, bipolar: **+ sympathetic** (adrenaline:
  rate and contractility climb over ~3 s), **− vagal** (rest/digest: rate
  drops almost immediately, ~0.35 s). The asymmetric lag is real
  physiology and gives slow "arousal" gestures vs fast "calm" gestures.
- **AFTERLOAD** — arterial resistance. High afterload: slower, shorter
  ejection, higher arterial baseline. Low: the pump empties freely.
- **CONTRACTILITY** — twitch amplitude (independent of fill).
- **COMPLIANCE** — ventricular wall softness. Low (stiff): small rapid
  fills and the **S4** sound (atrial kick thudding into a stiff wall).
  High (soft): big fills and the **S3** gallop sound in rapid filling.
- **VALVE** — bipolar disease knob: **− stenotic** (valves don't open
  well → turbulence murmurs where blood forces through) vs
  **+ regurgitant** (valves don't close well → backflow: volume is lost,
  arterial diastolic pressure sags, murmurs where pressure builds behind
  a leaking valve). At 0, healthy: clean thumps, no murmur.
- **HRV** — heart-rate variability depth. A bounded, band-limited wander
  recomputed every beat (nothing integrates, nothing accumulates); **0 =
  mathematically exactly periodic**.
- **IRRITABILITY** — ectopic-beat probability; **fully off at 0**. Above
  0, occasional premature beats fire early (never stacked — a refractory
  window prevents re-triggering, as in real myocardium). The sinus beat
  arriving too early is dropped, so the next intrinsic beat arrives after
  a **compensatory pause**, and the post-ectopic beat is **stronger** (it
  had extra time to fill — Frank–Starling again), a physically free
  musical accent.
- **SOUND LEVEL** — phonocardiogram output level.

## Patch recipes

### Two hearts, one circulation (the series loop)
```
heart A OUTFLOW ─▶ heart B FILL CV
heart B OUTFLOW ─▶ heart A FILL CV
```
Whatever A ejects arrives at B's atrium as filling, and vice versa. The
two clocks drift apart and then synchronize through Frank–Starling
compensation — the same self-balancing real hearts perform. Insert a
**Breath** VOLUME between the two links to stand in for the pulmonary
reservoir.

### Cardiorespiratory coupling (respiratory sinus arrhythmia)
```
Breath FLOW ─▶ Heart DRIVE CV
```
Real heart rates rise on inhale and fall on exhale. This one cable is the
whole phenomenon — breathing now gently "wobbles" the pulse, deeper
breaths = deeper wobble.

### The pump (sidechain ducking)
```
Heart OUTFLOW ─▶ (attenuate) ─▶ VCA CV on a sustained pad
```
Each beat ducks the pad by the actual stroke volume — fill the heart more
(FILL, DRIVE up) and the pump gets physically stronger.

### Swing clock
BEAT drives the sequencer; ATRIA (earlier by AV DELAY) drives the
offbeat. Turn AV DELAY to slide the shuffle.

### Slow-motion tension
MASS max (elephant) + RATE low: a 30 bpm heartbeat where every phase is
seconds long. VOLUME becomes a massive multi-second envelope; BEAT/ATRIA
become a deliberately dragging pulse.

## States (context-menu presets)

Shipped presets configure the *causes* (DRIVE/FILL/CONTRACTILITY), so the
rate emerges:

| Preset | Feel | What's set |
|---|---|---|
| Deep sleep | ~50 bpm, deep HRV wander | strong vagal DRIVE, low FILL, high HRV |
| Relaxed | ~65 bpm | mild vagal, low-normal FILL |
| Attentive | ~85 bpm | mild sympathetic, mid FILL |
| Light exercise | ~110 bpm, punchier | sympathetic, FILL up |
| Heavy exercise | ~170 bpm, maximal stroke | strong sympathetic, FILL + CONTRACTILITY maxed, AFTERLOAD dropped |

These work standalone on one heart with nothing patched. Coupled
behaviors (the loops above) are *patch* recipes — presets configure
organs; patches configure bodies. The preset files live in
`presets/cyberline-heart/` (Rack: right-click the module → **Factory
presets**).

## Panel reference

22 HP, four columns. Top: the **heart gauge** — atrium (top), ventricle
(bottom), aortic bulb (right), each drawn at its live volume/pressure and
colored by phase (cyan filling / warm ejecting / dim at rest). The two
**PHASE** lights duplicate this as LEDs: green = AV valve open (filling),
red = aortic valve open (ejecting).

| Column | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1 (anatomy) | MASS, RATE, AV DELAY, CONTRACT, COMPLIANCE, VALVE −/+, HRV |
| 2 (drive) | IRRIT, FILL, DRIVE, AFTERLOAD, SOUND level, PHASE lights |
| 3 (in → out) | PACING, FILL CV, DRIVE CV, AFT CV; VOLUME, PRESSURE, FLOW ± |
| 4 (out) | OUTFLOW, ARTERIAL, ECG, BEAT, ATRIA, SOUND |

## Notes & limits

- **Pacing**: a trigger into PACING fires a beat immediately and resets
  the internal clock — patch any clock/sequencer to "pace" the heart
  (overdrive pacing). Stop pacing and the intrinsic rhythm returns on its
  own (escape beats), like a real heart that's been paced.
- **Rate ceiling**: the physics block caps at 1000 bpm — hummingbird
  territory works, faster is refused (and physiologically irrelevant).
- **ECG is stylized-human** by design: real interspecies ECGs differ in
  wave shape; this module ships one honest waveform rather than a
  per-species table.
- No direct "envelope shape" or "thump pitch" controls: every shape and
  pitch is owned by the anatomy knobs. If it sounds wrong, fix the
  physiology, not the curve.
